## Known-gaps pass — 2026-10-13

**Status:** Implemented on branch `gaps-pass` and verified with headless Godot 4.7.2: the editor import passes, and all ten smoke tests pass (the new `gaps_smoke` has 102 checks). A screenshot tour under xvfb was inspected for the vale, camp, map, telegraphs, cloak close-ups and rift chambers. **Not tested on a phone.**

### What changed

- **Region:** `world._expand_ember()` appends 40 columns (biome 7, seed 20261013) after Starfall. It grows the biome and field maps and the shader arrays (`ground.gdshader` arrays [7] to [8]), and stamps the Ember Road, plaza, north and south roads and Amber Pond. Starfall's east wall skips rows 93 to 101, and Starfall props left on the finished road are removed. `_ember_place_actors` adds the autumn wall, camp props, the hollow ring, the grove, scatter, decor, 16 monsters plus an elite cyclope, 3 chests, a waystone, Rowan and the Emberbark. New prop kinds (`rock_orange`, `leaf_orange`, `ember_ore`) come from Ninja Adventure `nature.png`; `ember_ore` is breakable and gives Ore, with a 35% chance of an Ember Core. Biome clamps in `main.gd` (region subtitle, music, ambience) and the map palette now cover biome 7. Map labels near the east edge flip left.
- **Emberbark:** `DB.BOSSES["emberbark"]` uses `sheet` (reuses the giantbamboo frames), `swap` (palette swap via `boss.apply_palette`) and `side: true`. `main.is_side_boss` / `_side_boss_reward`: recorded in `defeated_bosses`, sets the flag `emberbark_felled`, pays 60 coins plus coin pickups and 2 Moon Shards, and is excluded from the Moonseed objective and reload Moonseed spawns. Journeys of type flag now complete immediately if the flag was earned before the journey began.
- **Telegraphs:** `fx.Telegraph` gains lane, cone and spokes shapes and draws true circles (previously squashed to 0.62 height while the damage check used a circle). `boss.gd`: lane matches the charge distance and contact width, the volley gets a wind-up and cone with the first burst along the cone, the ring warning uses the same offset as the shots, a "!" label shows while winding up, and `attack_pattern()` adds `quake` (r 64, 1.0s, 4 damage) when enraged.
- **Cloak:** `scripts/actors/cloak_layer.gd` is a child of the player, re-layered each frame with `_layer_behind_body`. Item `look` colour triples were added to every cloak, plus a new `emberleaf_mantle`. `visible_gear["cloak"]` is set while worn.
- **Rifts:** `rifts.tiled()` / `decal()` / `tile_texture()` (single tiles cut from the CC0 sheets, cached, repeated with `TEXTURE_REPEAT_ENABLED`), replacing the flat floor and wall colours.
- **Tests:** `tests/gaps_smoke.gd`. Updated counts in `adventure_slice_smoke` (5 bosses, 4 Moonseed keepers, 7 waystones) and `content_smoke` (344 x 176, 7 waystones). The screenshot tour gained `_gaps_shots()`.

### Not done / limits

- The Emberbark reuses the Briarback's animation sheet (recoloured); there is no new boss art.
- The cape is a procedural pixel layer with one sway frame, not hand-animated frames. Charms, rings and relics are still not drawn.
- The 8px reachability test approximates Mallow's collision; real walking in the vale was only spot-checked in screenshots.

## Open-world expansion — 2026-10-13

**Status:** Implemented on branch `open-world-expansion` and verified with headless Godot 4.7.2: the editor import/parse and all nine smoke tests pass (run one after another). A screenshot tour under xvfb was inspected for font clipping, HUD overlap, interiors, rifts, character creation and the trade screens. **Not tested on a phone** (touch feel, safe areas, performance).

### What changed

- **Combat input:** `player.attack_direction()` priority (stick > 0.35 wins; 50° assist cone snaps to the target; then lock, auto-target, facing); hold-to-repeat with a 0.22s buffer; removed the combo-cancel cooldown exploit, dash and the dodge button; perfect parry moved onto Petalguard's first 0.6s. Melee arcs check line of sight (`main.line_clear`, physics layer 1; low hedges are layer 4 and do not block).
- **Unarmed start:** `fists` weapon (weak 3-jab combo, small indicator, no sprite); `unequip_weapon()`; old saves get fists inserted first.
- **Visible gear:** `assets/shaders/hero_layers.gdshader` + `scripts/actors/hero_look.gd` recolour mask regions per worn item; masks generated by `tools/build_hero_masks.py`. Held weapon sprites (`refresh_held_weapon`, dual off-hand sprite) layered by child order so world props still cover them.
- **Character creation:** `scripts/ui/char_create.gd`, `main.begin_new_game(look)`; appearance saved.
- **Items/rarity:** new weapons (Bramble Spear, Cinder Axe, Petalbreaker, Moonwell Blade), armour (Starfall Crown, Moonwell Aegis, Riftwalker Treads, Hidebound Vest, Slimeglass Boots), relic charms (Gale Band → Swiftwind, Barkskin Charm → Barkskin), 11 materials, Moontea Vial, Rift Key. `scripts/ui/rarity_ui.gd` frames everywhere. Nightfang Ambush → capped Flurry.
- **Economy:** `scripts/data/economy.gd` (loot tables, gear pool + pity, recipes, shops, exchanges, recipe scrolls), `scripts/systems/inventory.gd`, `loot.gd`, `crafting.gd`, `trade.gd`; `scripts/ui/trade_screens.gd` BUY/SELL/TRADE/CRAFT. Satchel: rarity grid, filters, stack counts, bag capacity, UNEQUIP.
- **Quests:** `scripts/systems/quests.gd` — 17 journeys, chains, `matches()` / `marker_for()`; NPC markers in `npc.gd`; rewards paid once without coin pickups.
- **Rifts:** `scripts/systems/rifts.gd` (spawn rules, BFS reachability, tiers, three chambers, gates, Rift Warden, retreat/exit portals, cache once per uid, save/load, death handling). Map marker; minimap shows the entrance while inside.
- **World:** `main.POIS` — elite grounds and hidden caches placed by validated search; `RiftScript.make_elite` for elites. Chests no longer eat items when the bag is full and refund duplicates.
- **Houses:** interiors in `INTERIOR_POCKET`, camera bounds + hidden world, inner study, door cooldown, legacy-save mapping; dying indoors/in a rift returns to the waystone cleanly.
- **Knockback:** `scripts/combat/knockback.gd`.
- **Fonts:** Nunito + Pixelify Sans (OFL) via `scripts/ui/fonts.gd`; unsupported glyphs (✿ ▶ ✓ →) replaced with ASCII.
- **Tests:** new `tests/expansion_smoke.gd` (112 checks); older suites updated for the unarmed start, removed dash/dodge, Flurry and the character-creation step.

- Cloak, charm, ring and relic have no dedicated sprite art, so they are not drawn on the body.
- The map footprint is unchanged; new content is placed inside it.
- Rift chambers are flat-shaded rooms (no dedicated tileset yet).
- Some suites share a test save file and must not run in parallel.

## Petal Arts update — 2026-10-12

**Status:** All requested features implemented (attack target snap + range hint, softer knockback, enterable houses, quests, classes with combining and trees, equipment, Kaede's shop, the Hollow Crypt dungeon). **The headless checks have NOT been run in this environment** — it has no Godot binary — so they must pass before this leaves draft status. **This build has not been tested on a phone.**

### What changed

- **Combat feel:** `scripts/actors/player.gd` — basic melee (blade, hammer, Duskfan) now snaps toward the closest enemy in reach (`_snap_to_target`), and a faint pulsing ring (`RangeHint`) shows basic-attack range whenever a target hovers near. Knockback was re-tuned to feel weighty instead of launchy: blade 55/110, hammer 160, plunge 140, parry shockwave 130, mothlight 130, enemy-hits-Mallow 140 (down from 120–300).
- **Petal Arts (classes):** new `scripts/data/classdb.gd` — seven Albion-inspired lines (Warden/warrior, Pugilist/fighter, Nightbloom/assassin, Lumimancer/mage, Gravetender/necromancer, Windrunner/ranger, Petalweaver/cleric), each with two active arts and a four-node destiny-style tree. Kills grant XP (`add_xp`, cap 20); levels grant a point and focus (mana, regen 6/s). Bind a primary anytime, combine one secondary at level 5+ (max two lines, +1 attack synergy). Arts cast on keys 1-4 with focus costs and cooldowns; 14 archetypes implemented in `cast_skill` (strikes, flurries, volleys, piercing bolts, point bursts, blinks, buffs, heals, aoe rings, vampiric reaps). Critical hits double damage and roll from gear + tree + buffs.
- **Journeys (quests):** new `scripts/systems/quests.gd` — six quests with givers (Alder, Mira, Kit, Kaede, Lumen, Sora), objectives (kills, moonpetals, talk, boss, waystone, flag) and rewards (coins/XP/items). Talk to a giver to start, complete objectives through normal play, talk again to claim. HUD journey tracker line; H opens the Journeys log.
- **Enterable houses:** `scripts/core/main.gd` — four Hearthgrove homes open through door prompts; interiors are built at runtime on the quiet far-south sea (floor, walls, colliders, lights, furniture, doormats), with three interior villagers (Rosa, Jun, Old Fen) and a tea chest in the Elder's Hall. `scripts/world/world.gd` gained `interior_footprints` + `_clear_interiors()` so the ground shader, minimap and `is_water()` treat the rooms as land.
- **Hollow Crypt dungeon:** the graveyard door in Glasswater Marsh opens into a dungeon room with six keepers and **the Hollow Revenant** (a dungeon boss that bars the door until defeated). It drops the Moonveil Ring, 80 coins and the `crypt_cleared` flag — no Moonseed, no world-state change, no respawn. A map marker points at the crypt until cleared.
- **Equipment & shops:** new items in `scripts/data/db.gd` — Duskfan (weapon), Sunplate Vest (+2 defense), Moonveil Ring (+6% crit), Star Locket (+1 heart, +10 focus); defense reduces incoming damage. Kaede of the Watch now runs a shop (moontea, duskfan, sunplate, heart bloom); the nomad sells the Star Locket.
- **HUD:** `scripts/ui/hud.gd` — ARTS overlay (K) for binding lines and spending tree points, Journeys overlay (H), a 1-4 skill bar with icons and cooldown counts, XP and focus gauges, a journey tracker line, satchel lines for level/XP/focus/arts/journeys, and an ARTS top button. Controls: K arts, H journeys, 1-4 arts; K removed from attack, J removed from journeys to avoid collisions.
- **Save/load:** the save gains `xp`, `level`, `points`, `focus`, `classes`, `nodes`, `bar` and `quests` (all optional; older saves default cleanly).
- **Tests:** new `tests/growth_smoke.gd` covers levels, binding/combining, tree nodes, art casting, journeys lifecycle, Kaede's shop, houses, the crypt (including no-Moonseed and door-bar logic), overlays, target snap and knockback, and the save/load round-trip.
- **Docs:** README updated (classes, quests, houses, crypt, equipment, new controls, seven smoke tests).

## Open-world update — 2026-10-11

**Status:** A large upgrade to an open-world action adventure, as requested. The headless checks pass. **This build has not been tested on a phone**, so the Phase 1 real-device exit check is still outstanding.

### What changed

- **Scope:** The project moves from a single meadow slice to an open-world valley. `GAME_PLAN.md` is updated to match.
- **Assets:**
  - Imported 321 files from the CC0 **Ninja Adventure** pack (Pixel-boy & AAA): characters, monsters, bosses, tilesets, items, weapons, FX, UI, music and SFX. The import is reproducible with `tools/import_ninja_adventure.py`.
  - Added the CC0 **Kenney Mini/Pixel** fonts.
  - Credits are in `assets/packs/ASSET_CREDITS.md`.
- **Character:** Mallow is now a fully animated four-direction sprite, a NinjaGreen base recoloured rose/plum by shader palette swap. It has walk, attack, jump, item-get and dead poses, and outfit recolours for armour and boots.
- **Movement:**
  - Mallow can walk, sprint (with a stamina bar), dodge-roll (i-frames and afterimages), jump (hopping low props, with a landing plunge attack), swim, and push.
  - Dust, ripples and footstep SFX follow each move.
- **Combat:**
  - Weapons: Thornblade 3-hit combo, Bloomstaff homing bolt, Gale Bow charge shot, and Ironroot Hammer shockwave.
  - Skills: Mothlight, Petalguard and Moontea.
  - Hit feel: hit-stop, knockback, damage numbers, screen shake, flash and dissolve shaders.
  - Enemies: 19 monster types across five AI styles, with wind-up tells.
  - Bosses: four guardians with telegraphed multi-pattern fights, enrage, and a boss bar.
- **World:**
  - A seeded generator builds a 256×176-tile map with six biomes, roads, rivers and lakes.
  - It places about 2,080 collidable props, about 9,700 decor sprites, 171 monsters, villages, 13 chests, 5 waystones, shops and breakables.
  - Props are batched per chunk, and off-screen monsters are deactivated.
- **Rendering:**
  - New ground shader: biome palette blend, animated water, and shoreline foam.
  - Foliage wind shader, sprite FX shader, atmosphere layer (fog, god-rays, vignette), and post-process (soft bloom, grade, saturation).
  - Day/night cycle with 2D lights, plus regional weather.
  - The blight desaturation lifts as Moonseeds return.
  - A low-FX toggle is in Settings.
- **Systems:**
  - NPC dialogue and quests, shops, satchel with tap-to-equip, minimap and world map, and region banners.
  - Region music crossfades and ambient loops.
  - Save/load (`user://petalbound_save.json`, v2), autosave, waystone respawn, and the Moonwell ending.
- **Controls:**
  - Multi-touch buttons, including sprint/jump/roll and the context button.
  - Stick-edge auto-sprint.
  - Full keyboard and gamepad mappings.
  - Orientation changed to sensor landscape (`orientation=4`).
- **Visual QA fixes:**
  - Removed the `source_color` hints that linearised the palette.
  - Fixed double vertex-colour darkening.
  - Switched bloom to a luma threshold.
  - Toned down additive FX and night-light energy.
  - Added a majority filter that removes blocky biome islands.
