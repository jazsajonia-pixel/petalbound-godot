# First playable slice

## Direction inferred from the reference

- Landscape 2D top-down / slight three-quarter feel.
- Muted teal-green meadow, pale stone ruins wrapped in turquoise vines, and drifting pink petals.
- A small white, long-eared traveler with a dark plum cloak.
- A quick dash with a soft pale trail.

## Play loop

Explore a compact meadow, dash around low ruins, collect three glowing moonseeds, then reach the arch-shaped shrine. The on-screen buttons are for mobile play; keyboard controls are available for desktop testing.

This is intentionally a starter slice, not a complete story or a claim to reproduce every detail in the video. The world and pixel shapes are drawn in GDScript so the project is easy to clone and does not depend on external art packs.
